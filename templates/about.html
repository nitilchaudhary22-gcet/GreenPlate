About GreenPlate

Bridging the gap between food surplus and food scarcity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate edible food waste by providing a seamless, AI-driven platform that connects food donors like restaurants, hotels, and caterers directly with local NGOs and community shelters in real-time.

Our Vision

We envision a world where zero surplus food goes to landfills, every hunger spot is served efficiently through technology, and communities collaborate sustainably to feed more lives every day.

How GreenPlate Works

A simple 3-step process to save food and feed lives.

1

1. Restaurants Post Food

Food businesses post details of surplus meals including quantity, shelf-life, and pickup location.

2

2. AI Smart Match

Our AI matches the donation with the nearest verified NGO based on distance, quantity, and urgency.

3

3. Pickup & Deliver

The matched NGO receives a notification, picks up the fresh food, and distributes it to people in need.
